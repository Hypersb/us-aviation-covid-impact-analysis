U.S. AVIATION INDUSTRY - BEFORE VS. DURING COVID-19
====================================================

Project summary
---------------
This project is a comprehensive comparative analysis of U.S. commercial
aviation operations in 2019 and 2020.

Research question
-----------------
How did the COVID-19 pandemic coincide with changes in U.S. aviation
operations between 2019 and 2020, and which airlines, airports, routes, and
time periods experienced the greatest disruption?

This analysis is not simply a comparison of two datasets. It investigates an
industry-wide disruption through operational data.

Research dimensions
-------------------
1. Flight activity
   How did observed flight activity differ between 2019 and 2020?

2. Cancellations
   When did cancellations increase, and which airlines and airports
   experienced the highest rates?

3. Delays and reliability
   How did departure delays, arrival delays, and on-time performance change?

4. Airline performance
   Which carriers experienced the largest operational changes?

5. Airport and route networks
   Which origin-destination routes and airports showed the largest changes
   in sampled activity and reliability?

Important data limitation
-------------------------
The current datasets are samples, not the complete national flight
population. Actual nationwide flight-volume changes cannot be estimated
without complete official flight counts or a verified representative sample.

Real-world purpose
------------------
This project is designed as an aviation analytics study for organizations
such as airlines, airport authorities, aviation consulting companies, network
planning teams, and risk management teams.

Potential business applications
-------------------------------

Airline executives - operational resilience
  - Identify periods with severe cancellations.
  - Compare operational changes across carriers.
  - Evaluate how operational reliability changed during the crisis.
  - Support disruption planning and operational contingency strategies.

Airport authorities - capacity planning
  - Analyze airports with major changes in sampled activity.
  - Compare cancellation and delay patterns.
  - Inform staffing, gate allocation, and contingency planning.

Network planners - route analysis
  - Compare origin-destination route activity.
  - Evaluate route-level operational reliability.
  - Identify vulnerable routes for future network planning.

Risk management teams - crisis preparedness
  - Study the timing and severity of operational disruption.
  - Analyze operational changes before and during the crisis.
  - Develop more informed responses to future emergencies.

These are potential uses of the analysis, not benefits claimed to have been
achieved without implementation and validation.

What we are building
--------------------
We are building an Aviation Impact Intelligence Dashboard: a polished,
multi-page analytics application for comparing U.S. aviation operations
between 2019 and 2020.

Dashboard period
  2019-2020

Dashboard sections
------------------

01. Executive Overview
    Key metrics and year-over-year changes.

02. Flight Activity
    Monthly trends and activity patterns.

03. Cancellation Intelligence
    Cancellation timing, rates, and reasons.

04. Airline Comparison
    Carrier performance and rankings.

05. Airport and Route Analysis
    Origin, destination, and route-level patterns.

06. Delays and Reliability
    Departure, arrival, and on-time performance.

Dashboard capabilities
---------------------

Executive KPI overview
  - Total sampled flights.
  - Total cancellations.
  - Cancellation rates.
  - Average delays.
  - On-time percentage.
  - Year-over-year changes.

Monthly trend comparison
  - Compare 2019 and 2020 monthly activity.
  - Identify the months with the greatest observed changes.
  - Show year-over-year differences.

Airline comparison
  - Search for and select airlines.
  - Compare airline activity, cancellations, delays, and reliability.
  - Rank carriers by operational performance and change.

Airport comparison
  - Find airports with the highest cancellation rates.
  - Find airports with the highest delay rates.
  - Compare sampled airport activity between the two years.

Route explorer
  - Examine origin-destination pairs.
  - Compare route activity between 2019 and 2020.
  - Evaluate route-level reliability and disruption patterns.

Cancellation reasons
  - Compare recorded cancellation categories.
  - Analyze when and where cancellation reasons changed.

Delay distribution
  - Explore average and median delays.
  - Identify severe delays.
  - Compare departure and arrival delay patterns.

Interactive filters
  - Year.
  - Month.
  - Airline.
  - Origin.
  - Destination.

Metric rules
-----------
- Distinguish cancelled flights from completed flights.
- Do not count missing delay measurements as zero.
- Calculate delay and reliability metrics only from flights with valid
  measurements.
- Clearly label metrics based on sampled data.
- Preserve the distinction between observed flight activity and complete
  national flight volume.

Analysis goals
--------------
The analysis should answer questions such as:

- Which months had the greatest observed cancellation-rate increases
  compared with the same months in 2019?
- Which carriers and airports contributed to the observed patterns?
- Did on-time performance improve or worsen among flights that actually
  operated?
- Which routes experienced the greatest changes in sampled activity and
  reliability?
- Which periods show the clearest operational disruption?

Evidence standards
------------------
Every major conclusion should be supported by:

1. A reproducible query or calculation.
2. A chart or table.
3. A numerical result.
4. A clear explanation of the relevant limitation.

Important interpretation note
-----------------------------
Improved on-time performance among surviving flights does not necessarily
mean that the aviation system became more reliable overall. Cancellations and
changes in which flights operated can affect the comparison and should be
considered when interpreting reliability results.
